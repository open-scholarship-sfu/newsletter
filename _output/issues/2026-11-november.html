Creative Knowledge Mobilization Workshop Series

The SFU Community-Engaged Research Initiative, the SFU Knowledge Mobilization Hub and the UBC Public Humanities Hub are hosting a series of workshops exploring creative approaches to knowledge mobilization. Designed for researchers, graduate students, practitioners and community partners, the series introduces practical skills for translating research into engaging, accessible and impactful public-facing formats. On November 10, the theme is podcasting!

Celebrating Open Education at SFU

Open educational resources (OER) and practices (OEP) make learning more affordable, accessible and open for SFU students. On November 10, from 12:30 to 2:00 p.m., join us for a celebration recognizing SFU instructors and practitioners who are using, developing and supporting OER and OEP for teaching and learning!

Whether you’re already using OER in your teaching, supporting open education in your work, or curious about getting started, you’re warmly invited to celebrate SFU’s growing open education community.

The event is hosted by the Open Education Community of Practice and Open Scholarship Framework teams here at SFU. Coffee and light refreshments will be provided.

Open Scholarship Champions: new round open!

We’re still accepting applications for the Center for Open Science’s self-paced courses! If you want to become an Open Scholarship Champion, we will offer you a full scholarship to complete one or more of these training courses and get a certificate.

Grantees have until May 1, 2027 to complete their training and until August 1, 2027 to complete a project of their choice.

If you’re unable or prefer not to take a COS course, you can still participate! Simply complete another open scholarship training and provide proof of completion. All participants, whichever training they choose, have the same August 1, 2027 deadline for their projects.

Apply for a COS seat Register as a Champion

This opportunity is recognized on the Co-Curricular Record (CCR), an official university document that tracks your co-curricular involvement at SFU. Learn more about the Co-Curricular Record.

New open killer whale sound catalogue

From SFU News:

“A new open-access catalogue of whale calls will help researchers and orca enthusiasts identify distinct killer whale populations, clans and family groups by their vocalizations alone. Developed at Simon Fraser University, the Orca Call Catalogue serves as a field guide to killer whale calls, pulling decades of recordings into a searchable online library for the first time, says John Ford, scientist emeritus at Fisheries and Oceans Canada.”

This project shows how engaged our community is in open scholarship practices! Shout out to the Research Computing Group for providing the infrastructure needed for this project.

If you have a project that you’d like to make open, reach out to us and we’ll help you figure out a plan to make it happen!
